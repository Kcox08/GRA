Southern Butter Pound Cake

Southern Butter Pound Cake is a rich, buttery classic with a golden crust and a tender, moist crumb. Baked with simple, comforting ingredients, every slice offers a smooth vanilla flavor and melt-in-your-mouth texture. Delicious on its own or served with fresh berries, whipped cream, or a warm glaze.

Ingredints

Pound Cake

  • all-purpose flour
  • baking powder
  • salt
  • unsalted butter, room temperature
  • butter-flavored shortening
  • granulated sugar
  • eggs, room temperature
  • egg yolks, room temperature
  • vanilla extract
  • lemon extract
  • natural butter flavoring, or butter extract (optional, but it enhances the buttery taste)
  • whole milk, room temperature
  • whole milk, room temperature

    Glaze

  • 1 cup sifted powdered sugar
  • 2–3 tablespoons milk or heavy cream
  • Pinch of salt (optional for balance)

Steps

Pound Cake

  1. Preheat oven to 325 ° F. Grease and flour a bundt pan. Set aside.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.
  3. In a large bowl, cream together butter, shortening, and sugar for 3 minutes.
  4. Mix in eggs, one at a time, mixing thoroughly after each egg.
  5. Mix in egg yolks.
  6. Mix in vanilla extract, lemon extract, and butter flavoring or butter extract (if using)
  7. Add dry ingredients to wet ingredients, alternating with the milk and buttermilk.
  8. Mix until the batter is fluffy.
  9. Spoon batter into prepared pan and tap the pan on the counter to even out the top and release any air bubbles.
  10. Bake for 1 hour and 20 mins. (Check it at the 1-hour mark. Do not over-bake as the cake will continue to cook as it cools)
  11. Remove from the oven and let the cake sit in the pan for about 10 minutes.
  12. Then remove from the pan and place on a cooling rack to finish cooling.
  13. Slice and serve!

    Glaze

  1. Sift the powdered sugar into a bowl to remove lumps.
  2. Add milk and vanilla and whisk until smooth and glossy. Start with 2 tablespoons of liquid and add more, a teaspoon at a time, until the glaze is thick but pourable
  3. Cool the pound cake completely before glazing to prevent the glaze from running off
  4. Pour the glaze over the cake, letting it drip naturally down the sides. For a thicker coat, allow the first layer to set for 20 minutes, then apply a second layer
